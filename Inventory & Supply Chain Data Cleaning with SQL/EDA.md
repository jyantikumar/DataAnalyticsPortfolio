1. Total Revenue:

---

# 👥 Customer Analysis

These help Marketing and Sales understand customers.

1. Who are the top 10 customers by revenue?
2. Which customer type generates the most revenue?
3. Which cities generate the highest revenue?
4. How many repeat customers are there?
5. Which customers have never made a purchase?
6. Average spend per customer.
7. New customers by month.
8. Which customer purchased the greatest variety of products?

---

# 📦 Product Analysis

These are questions Product Managers usually ask.

1. Top 10 best-selling products (by quantity).
2. Top 10 highest revenue products.
3. Lowest-selling products.
4. Products that have never been sold.
5. Revenue by category.
6. Average selling price by category.
7. Which category has the highest revenue?
8. Which products generate the highest profit (if you calculate profit)?

---

# 🏭 Inventory Analysis

These questions are common for warehouse teams.

1. Current stock by warehouse.
2. Total inventory value by warehouse.
3. Products below reorder level.
4. Products with zero stock.
5. Products with negative stock (if any).
6. Products not updated for a long time.
7. Warehouse with the highest inventory value.
8. Warehouse with the largest number of unique products.

---

# 🚚 Supplier Analysis

Questions Procurement would ask.

1. Number of products supplied by each supplier.
2. Suppliers generating the highest revenue.
3. Suppliers with inactive products.
4. Suppliers whose products have never sold.
5. Average product cost by supplier.
6. Supplier distribution by city.

---

# 💰 Sales Analysis

These are probably the most important.

1. Monthly revenue.
2. Daily revenue.
3. Revenue by payment method.
4. Revenue by sales representative.
5. Number of orders by status.
6. Cancelled vs Returned vs Completed orders.
7. Highest-value sales transaction.
8. Lowest-value sales transaction.

---

# 📈 Operational Insights

These are the kinds of questions Operations Managers love.

1. Which products should be reordered?
2. Which products are overstocked?
3. Which products sell quickly but have low inventory?
4. Which products have high inventory but low sales?
5. Which warehouse needs replenishment the most?
6. Which warehouse contributes the most revenue?

---

# 🔍 Data Quality Report

At the end of your cleaning project, document:

- Total rows before cleaning (per table).
- Total rows after cleaning.
- Duplicates removed.
- Missing values found.
- Invalid values corrected.
- Values standardized.
- Business assumptions made.
- Remaining issues left for investigation.

# 🌟 Bonus Questions (Senior-Level)

These aren't required, but they'll make your portfolio stand out because they require combining multiple tables and thinking beyond simple aggregations.

1. **ABC Analysis (Pareto Principle)**  
    Classify products into:
    - A: Top 80% of revenue
    - B: Next 15%
    - C: Remaining 5%
2. **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)**  
    Based on the available sales data, who are the most valuable customers?
3. **Inventory Turnover**  
    Which products move quickly versus those that sit in inventory?
4. **Supplier Performance**  
    Which suppliers contribute the most revenue, and which contribute the least?
5. **Revenue vs Inventory Value**  
    Are expensive products actually generating revenue, or are they tying up inventory?
